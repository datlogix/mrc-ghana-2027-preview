What is MRC?

Robotics, played as a sport.

The Minoan RobotSports Competition turns engineering into athletics. Students design, build and program robots and drones that race, shoot, play football and fly precision missions — under clear rules, against the clock and against each other.

MRC is an international programme of the Hellenic Educational Robotics Organization (H.E.R.O.). In Ghana, it is organised by The MakersPlace.

International pathway

From Accra to Crete.

MRC Ghana 2027 is part of a worldwide robot sports movement that meets each year at the MRC Global Olympiad in Greece.

Any progression from Ghana to Greece depends on official eligibility, national selection arrangements, compliance with the rules and any international places confirmed. Winning a Ghana category does not by itself guarantee selection, and selection does not include travel or fees unless separately confirmed.

When and where is MRC Ghana 2027?+

MRC Ghana 2027 takes place on Friday 29 and Saturday 30 January 2027 in Accra, Ghana. The venue will be announced once confirmed.

Which sports can we enter?+

Seven sports are on the MRC Ghana 2027 programme: Drones, Drone Soccer 3×3, Marathon, Football 3×3, Shooting, Archery and Exhibition. Each has its own page with a summary and a link to the official H.E.R.O. rules.

Who can take part?+

Participation is based on age, not school class. For the six sports with published rules, all team members must be at least 10 years old and are placed in age categories (for example Kids 10–12, Teens/Senior 12+–18 and Adults 18+; Marathon also separates Teens 12+–15 and Senior 15+–18). How exact ages are calculated will be confirmed by the organisers. Exhibition eligibility is awaiting official rules.

How do we register?+

Use the Register a Team button. It takes you to the official MRC Ghana 2027 registration application, where you sign in with your email, add your organisation, team and robot details, and submit an expression of interest. The MakersPlace then reviews each entry against the current H.E.R.O. rules and contacts you.

Can Ghanaian teams go to the Global Olympiad in Greece?+

The MRC Global Olympiad 2027 takes place on 23–26 April 2027 in Chania, Crete, Greece. Any progression from Ghana depends on official eligibility, the national selection arrangements, compliance with the rules and any international places confirmed. Winning a Ghana category does not by itself guarantee selection.
