MRC Ghana 2027 set for 29–30 January in Accra

The Minoan RobotSports Competition Ghana 2027 will take place on Friday 29 and Saturday 30 January 2027 in Accra. Here is what we know so far and how to get involved.

MRC Ghana 2027 team·15 October 2026·1 min read

MRC Ghana 2027 will take place on Friday 29 and Saturday 30 January 2027 in Accra, Ghana. The venue will be announced once it is confirmed.

Please note the dates. If you saw earlier dates for MRC Ghana 2027, please use 29–30 January 2027.

What is MRC Ghana 2027?

MRC Ghana 2027 is Ghana's edition of the Minoan RobotSports Competition, where robotics is played as a sport. Students design, build and program robots and drones that race, shoot, fly precision missions and play football, all under clear rules and against the clock.

The competition is organised in Ghana by The MakersPlace. The Minoan RobotSports Competition is an international programme of the Hellenic Educational Robotics Organization (H.E.R.O.).

Seven sports

The MRC Ghana 2027 programme includes seven sports:

  • Drones
  • Drone Soccer 3×3
  • Marathon
  • Football 3×3
  • Shooting
  • Archery
  • Exhibition

Each sport has its own page with a plain-English summary, a preparation checklist, a downloadable information sheet and a link to the official H.E.R.O. rules.

How to take part

  1. Choose a sport and check the published age bands.
  2. Read the official rules and form your team.
  3. Register your team. Registration is an expression of interest: The MakersPlace reviews every entry against the current H.E.R.O. rules and contacts the applicant.

Still to be announced

The venue, the daily timetable, registration fees and deadlines will be published on this website as soon as they are confirmed. See the Programme & venue page for updates.

Questions? Contact us.
