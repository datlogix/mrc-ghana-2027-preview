<p><strong>Please note the dates.</strong> If you saw earlier dates for MRC Ghana 2027, please use <strong>29–30 January 2027</strong>.</p>

<h2>What is MRC Ghana 2027?</h2>
<p>MRC Ghana 2027 is Ghana&#39;s edition of the Minoan RobotSports Competition, where robotics is played as a sport. Students design, build and program robots and drones that race, shoot, fly precision missions and play football, all under clear rules and against the clock.</p>
<p>The competition is organised in Ghana by <strong>The MakersPlace</strong>. The Minoan RobotSports Competition is an international programme of the <strong>Hellenic Educational Robotics Organization (H.E.R.O.)</strong>.</p>
<h2>Seven sports</h2>
<p>The MRC Ghana 2027 programme includes seven sports:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/categories/drones">Drones</a></li>
<li><a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/categories/drone-soccer">Drone Soccer 3×3</a></li>
<li><a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/categories/marathon">Marathon</a></li>
<li><a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/categories/football">Football 3×3</a></li>
<li><a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/categories/shooting">Shooting</a></li>
<li><a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/categories/archery">Archery</a></li>
<li><a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/categories/exhibition">Exhibition</a></li>
</ul>
<p>Each sport has its own page with a plain-English summary, a preparation checklist, a downloadable information sheet and a link to the official H.E.R.O. rules.</p>
<h2>How to take part</h2>
<ol>
<li>Choose a sport and check the published age bands.</li>
<li>Read the official rules and form your team.</li>
<li><a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/register">Register your team</a>. Registration is an expression of interest: The MakersPlace reviews every entry against the current H.E.R.O. rules and contacts the applicant.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Still to be announced</h2>
<p>The venue, the daily timetable, registration fees and deadlines will be published on this website as soon as they are confirmed. See the <a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/programme">Programme &amp; venue</a> page for updates.</p>
