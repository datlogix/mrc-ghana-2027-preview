21:T73f,<p>The Minoan RobotSports Competition is an international movement that meets each year at the <strong>MRC Global Olympiad</strong> in Greece, organised by the Hellenic Educational Robotics Organization (H.E.R.O.).</p>
<h2>Global Olympiad 2027 at a glance</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dates:</strong> 23–26 April 2027</li>
<li><strong>Location:</strong> Chania, Crete, Greece</li>
<li><strong>Venue:</strong> Chania Kladissos Indoor Hall</li>
<li><strong>Edition:</strong> 6th MRC Global Olympiad</li>
</ul>
<p>Source: <a href="https://www.he-ro.gr/minoan-robotsports-competition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">he-ro.gr</a>, checked 14 October 2026.</p>
<h2>Two separate events</h2>
<p><strong>MRC Ghana 2027</strong> (29–30 January 2027, Accra) and the <strong>MRC Global Olympiad 2027</strong> (23–26 April 2027, Chania) are separate events with their own organisers and arrangements.</p>
<h2>Is there a pathway from Ghana to Greece?</h2>
<p>Any progression from Ghana to the Global Olympiad is <strong>conditional</strong>. It depends on the official eligibility rules, the national selection arrangements, compliance with the H.E.R.O. rules and any international places confirmed for Ghana.</p>
<p>Winning a category at MRC Ghana 2027 <strong>does not by itself guarantee selection</strong>. Selection also does not include flights, accommodation, international registration fees or sponsorship unless this is separately confirmed in writing.</p>
<p>Details of the national selection process will be published on this website. Read more on the <a href="/mrc-ghana-2027-preview/global-finals">Global Olympiad pathway</a> page.</p>
