Competition categories

Seven sports. Pick your challenge.

Each category has its own page with a plain-English summary, preparation checklist, downloadable information sheet and a link to the official H.E.R.O. rules.

Ghana programme confirmation

These seven sports form the MRC Ghana 2027 working programme. Final participation arrangements are subject to confirmation by the Ghana organising committee.

  • Drones

    Precision piloting missionsTeams pilot a small indoor drone through a secret course of hoops, obstacles and landing pads, scoring points for precision and order.2–3 team membersDetails

  • Drone Soccer 3×3

    Alliance aerial footballAlliances of three caged 'drone balls' play aerial football in a netted arena — only the Golden Ball drone can score through the opponents' hoop.2–3 team membersDetails

  • Marathon

    Autonomous line-following raceAn autonomous robot follows a black line around a secret track. The fastest clean run within three minutes wins. Basic and Advanced divisions.2–3 team membersDetails

  • Football 3×3

    Remote-controlled robot footballAlliances of three remote-controlled robots play five-minute football matches, using motorised kicking mechanisms to score goals.2–3 team membersDetails

  • Shooting

    Robotic foam-dart precisionTeams build a handheld, sensor-triggered foam-dart launcher and aim for three scoring targets at two distances.2–3 team membersDetails

  • Archery

    Autonomous robotic bowAn autonomous robot bow adjusts its own aim and fires a team-made, blunt-tipped arrow at a ten-ring target from four distances.2–3 team membersDetails

  • Exhibition

    Innovation showcaseA showcase category for original robotics and engineering projects. Detailed H.E.R.O. rules for 2027 are awaiting publication.Team size TBCDetails

Drones vs Drone Soccer

Drones is a precision-piloting mission flown by one team at a time. Drone Soccer 3×3 is an aerial football match between alliances of three teams. They are separate sports with separate rules.

Age-based eligibility

Eligibility is based on age, not school class. See each category for its published age bands. Read the FAQs.
