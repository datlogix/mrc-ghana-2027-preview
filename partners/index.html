Partnerships & sponsorship

Partner With Us to Build Ghana's Next Generation of Innovators

MRC Ghana 2027 gives young people hands-on experience in robotics, engineering and digital skills — and a route to international competition. Partners make that possible.
Become a SponsorExplore Partnership OpportunitiesRequest the Sponsorship Prospectus

Where you can help

Partnership opportunity areas

Every partnership is shaped with you. These are the areas where support makes the greatest difference.
  • 01

    Competition venue & infrastructure

    Help secure and equip a professional venue in Accra for two days of national competition.

  • 02

    Robotics equipment & arenas

    Support competition arenas, fields, timing systems and robotics kits that meet official specifications.

  • 03

    Student participation & inclusion

    Enable schools and young people from under-served communities to take part, including girls in STEM.

  • 04

    Technical & volunteer capacity

    Contribute engineers, judges, mentors and volunteers who bring the competition to life.

  • 05

    Awards & recognition

    Celebrate excellence through medals, trophies and recognition for students, coaches and schools.

  • 06

    Media & communications

    Help tell the story of Ghana's young innovators through broadcast, digital and print media.

  • 07

    International team preparation & travel

    Support teams preparing for international competition, should they be selected under the official process.

  • 08

    In-kind equipment & professional services

    Offer products, connectivity, logistics, printing, catering or professional expertise.

Why partner

Visible impact, measurable skills.

Young talent

Connect with students, coaches and schools building practical engineering and coding skills.

National visibility

Be associated with a national robotics championship in Accra.

International connection

Support a programme linked to the international MRC Global Olympiad.

Purposeful engagement

Offer staff volunteering, mentoring and technical expertise.
